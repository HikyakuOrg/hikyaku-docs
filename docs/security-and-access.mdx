# Access Control

Access control decides what each person on your team can see and do. You set it per person, from **Fleet → Team members**.

Everything is scoped to one organisation. If someone belongs to two organisations, their access in one says nothing about their access in the other.

:::warning[Choosing a role does not grant any access]

A role is a label. On its own it gives a team member nothing — not even the ability to see a package.

Access comes from the **permission checkboxes** you tick when adding or inviting someone. If you set a role and skip the checkboxes, that person signs in to an empty dashboard.

Changing someone's role later does not change their permissions either. You have to update those separately.

:::

## Roles

| Role | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Driver** | The only role with built-in behaviour. Creates a driver profile — licence number, expiry, country of issue, probation status, licence type — and makes the person available for assignment and the mobile app. |
| **Manager**, **Dispatcher**, **Picker**, **Technician** | Labels for organising your team list. They do not affect access. |
| **Admin** | Not something you assign. Anyone holding every permission is shown as an admin automatically. |

## Permissions

Permissions are named `resource.action` and grouped by resource in the picker, with a select-all toggle for each group.

| Group | Permissions | What it covers |
|---|---|---|
| **packages** | `view` `add` `update` `delete` | Packages, dimensions, delivery windows, tracking history, proof of delivery, and failure records |
| **shifts** | `view` `assign` | Assigning packages to drivers, and running and viewing route optimisation |
| **drivers** | `view` `add` `update` `delete` | Driver profiles and licence details |
| **vehicles** | `view` `add` `update` `delete` | Vehicles, plus fuel cards, card transactions, payout setup, and service rates |
| **customers** | `view` `add` `update` `delete` | Customer records |
| **warehouse** | `view` `add` `update` `delete` | Warehouse service entries |
| **locations** | `view` | Live driver locations and location history |
| **team_members** | `view` `add` `edit` `delete` | Adding, editing, deactivating, and reactivating team members and invitations |
| **service_areas** | `edit` | Creating and editing service areas. Every member can already view them |
| **organisation** | `edit` | Organisation settings |

:::note[Permissions that do not do anything yet]

`service_rates.*`, `fuel_cards.*`, and `transactions.view` appear in the picker but are not active. Fuel cards, transactions, and service rates are currently controlled by the **vehicles** permissions instead. Ticking these has no effect.

:::

## Adding a team member

1. Go to **Fleet → Team members** and choose **Add Team Member**.
2. Enter their details and pick a **Role**. Choosing Driver adds the licence fields.
3. Tick the permissions they need. **Do not skip this step** — see the warning above.
4. Submit. They get an email invitation, and their access applies from their first sign-in.

:::note[You can only grant permissions you hold yourself]

This applies whether you add someone directly or send them an invitation. Ticking a permission you do not hold is refused. *You cannot grant permissions you do not hold* and nothing is created: no account, no invitation, no email.

So a member with `team_members.add` can pass on any part of their own access, but cannot exceed it. To give someone access you do not have yourself, ask a full-permission account to add them.

:::

The person who created the organisation holds every permission. That is the account to keep safe.

## Starting points

Sensible sets to adjust rather than copy blindly.

| Job | Permissions |
|---|---|
| **Driver** | None. See below. |
| **Dispatcher** | `packages.view` `packages.add` `packages.update` · `shifts.view` `shifts.assign` · `drivers.view` · `vehicles.view` · `locations.view` · `customers.view` |
| **Warehouse / picker** | `packages.view` `packages.update` · `warehouse.view` `warehouse.add` `warehouse.update` · `customers.view` |
| **Fleet technician** | `vehicles.view` `vehicles.update` · `drivers.view` |
| **Operations manager** | The dispatcher set, plus `team_members.*`, `service_areas.edit`, and `organisation.edit` |

**Managing drivers without full team access.** Give a dispatcher `drivers.add`, `drivers.update`, or `drivers.delete` and they can add, edit, and remove team members — but only people whose role is Driver. They gain no authority over managers or other dispatchers. Prefer this over `team_members.*` for anyone who only runs the driver roster.

## What drivers get automatically

A driver needs no permissions at all. Once a package is assigned to them, they can:

- See that package, its customer, and the vehicle they are using
- Record proof of delivery, delivery failures, and tracking updates on it
- Share their own location while working
- View their own profile and location history

They see nothing else — no other driver's work, and no package that is not theirs. Grant permissions to a driver only if you want them to see beyond their own assignments.

## Changing and removing access

**Adjusting permissions** — update them from the team member's record. Changes apply on their next action.

**Deactivating someone** blocks sign-in and ends their active sessions. Nothing is deleted: their delivery history and records stay intact, and reactivating restores access.

Two deactivations are refused:

- Your own account.
- An account holding every permission. This is what stops the last administrator being locked out. Remove a permission from that account first if you genuinely need to deactivate it.

Deactivating several people at once processes each one separately, so some can succeed while others fail. Check the result rather than assuming all of them worked.

## Tips

- **Grant the narrowest useful action.** Someone who needs to see packages rarely needs to delete them.
- **Keep two accounts with full permissions.** Full-permission accounts cannot be deactivated, but they can still be lost — a second one is your way back in.
- **`team_members.add` still warrants care.** Holders cannot create an account with more access than they have themselves, but they can hand out every permission they do hold.
- **Revisit access when someone changes job.** Changing their role does nothing on its own.
